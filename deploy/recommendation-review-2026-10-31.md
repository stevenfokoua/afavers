# Recommendation review — 31 October 2026

## Evidence and method

The live anonymous public API returned **877 active public records** at 09:32 UTC. An ownership-checked, runner-wrapped `BEGIN READ ONLY` database export at 09:34 UTC added public descriptions/language and anonymous aggregate settings groups. No production record, preference, account or schedule was changed. The analysis uses the existing saved discovery criteria, which are deliberately omitted here; the stated lens remains GIS/environment and overlapping technical work. No qualifications, language proficiency or seniority are assumed.

The sample below contains **21 actual advertised vacancies plus one separately identified unsolicited-application control**. All 22 were in that live public snapshot and have source-linked BA pages reviewed. This is a purposive diagnostic sample, not a random market survey or measured recommendation-accuracy estimate. Some research-page snapshots were cached for up to two weeks; their relative “published X days ago” text is not a live timestamp. Application availability still needs checking with the employer.

## Measured findings

- All 877 descriptions were title-derived `Position:` summaries; median length 64 characters. None contained complete requirements or a stored deadline.
- 230 records had source posting dates within 7 days, 497 within 30 days, 242 older than 90 days. Posting dates were present and non-future for all 877. A recent import/last-seen timestamp does not reset posting age.
- Six records contain explicit GIS evidence in the captured title/summary. Source bodies reveal additional GIS work, including the Erftverband and Vössing roles. This is a concrete recall gap from summary-only ingestion.
- 732 stored language values were unknown; 24 said English. **None of the captured summaries actually confirms a working language.** German descriptions can contain English job titles.
- Under unchanged criteria, 96 records initially scored 70 or above. Replaying the revised scorer against the same un-enriched snapshot yields 0: title-only summaries are capped below 70, synonymous preferences count once, and unsupported English bonuses disappear. This deliberately limits high-relevance claims while full details are collected; ordinary browsing and keyword search remain available. These are pre-enrichment replay results, not post-deployment acceptance.

## Market calibration and product decisions

NRW reported 7.8% unemployment and 160,615 registered vacancies in October, but new registrations were 7.3% below the prior October. The national 2025 shortage analysis includes surveying, while software development and IT sales are no longer classified as shortage occupations at that aggregate level. These figures justify checking geospatial/infrastructure demand where it overlaps the existing search; they do not justify a blanket software bonus or redirecting someone into an unrelated regulated profession. Sources: [BA NRW October release](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/rd-nrw/presse/2026-24-nrw-arbeitsmarkt-oktober-2026-stabile-herbstbelebung-aber-noch-kein-aufschwung), [BA shortage analysis release](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/presse/2026-32-fachkraefte-bleiben-knapp-zuwanderung-gewinnt-weiter-an-bedeutung). Both primary releases were checked during this review; machine-readable context is in `deploy/market-context-2026-10-31.json`.

Implemented follow-up: shared word/phrase matching preserves real GIS/IT evidence; semantic synonyms cannot inflate one concept repeatedly; source-data limitations are separate from missing saved criteria; title-only summaries cannot claim high relevance; legacy title-language tags confer no English bonus; and the UI/email use `/100 relevance` with original-listing checks. The psychology record drops 84→62 without inventing a personal disqualification. Broad counselling/consulting terms can still discover different domains because saved criteria are preserved.

Bounded BA detail enrichment is a separate coordinated change: official v6 search returns summaries, the tested v6 detail route rejects access, and the official v4 detail route returns full advert text. Full descriptions, source deadlines and evidence quality should be retained when later search-only refreshes arrive. Enrichment must remain inside the worker’s shared 85-second request budget; its coverage and pending summaries should be reported honestly.

Outstanding acceptance: run a fresh import and repeat the cohort after enriched bodies/deadlines reach production; confirm the Duisburg/C1/deadline and body-only GIS cases; verify default-threshold alerts resume with eligible enriched listings. No applicant ground truth, employer closure confirmation, physical-device acceptance or post-enrichment ranking measurement is claimed. All 877 captured records came from Bundesagentur; this sample does not establish coverage of other providers or the whole German job market.
